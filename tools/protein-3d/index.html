WebGL / 2D Canvas 3D Graphics

Interactive 3D Biomolecular Structure Viewer

Zero-dependency client-side 3D renderer. Computes real-time 3D affine matrix transformations, depth-sorting, perspective projections, and directional shading to display atomic chains and ribbon backbones in your browser.

Human Insulin (1COH Model) Interactive 3D: Click & Drag to Rotate · Scroll to Zoom

🕹️ Controls & Rendering Architecture

  • Rotation: Click and drag anywhere on the 3D canvas with your mouse (or drag with your finger on touchscreens) to rotate azimuth and elevation angles.
  • Zoom: Scroll your mouse wheel or pinch-to-zoom to smoothly adjust focal distance and scale.
  • Presets: Toggle between Human Insulin hexamer/monomer coordinates (1COH), idealized right-handed $\alpha$-helix ($3.6$ residues per turn, $1.5$ Å rise), and canonical B-DNA double helix.
🔒 Private Data & Pipelines AlphaFold DB Coordinate Fetching & mmCIF Parsers

Production Python tools (starter_kit/protein_3d_metrics.py) parsing AlphaFold per-residue pLDDT matrices and generating publication-ready matplotlib 3D backbones are stored in our private repository.
