🔬 Biophysical Background: Kyte & Doolittle Scale (1982)

The Kyte-Doolittle scale assigns a hydropathy value to each of the 20 natural amino acids based on water-vapor transfer free energies and interior-versus-exterior distribution in known protein crystal structures. Values range from Isoleucine (+4.5) and Valine (+4.2) down to Arginine (-4.5) and Lysine (-3.9).

$$H_i = \frac{1}{2w + 1} \sum_{j=-w}^{w} h_{i+j}$$

When utilizing a window size of 19–21 residues (characteristic length of an alpha helix traversing a lipid bilayer), any continuous segment scoring above +1.6 strongly indicates a transmembrane spanning domain.
