Genomic GC-Skew Analyzer

Calculate $(C-G)/(C+G)$ sliding-window asymmetry profiles, AT-skew, nucleotide counts, and identify bacterial replication origins ($oriC$).

Kyte-Doolittle Hydropathy Profile

Compute continuous amino acid hydrophobicity indices over adjustable sliding window radii $(2w+1)$ to pinpoint transmembrane segments.
