🧬 Scientific Theory & Mathematical Formulation

In circular bacterial chromosomes, DNA replication initiates bidirectionally at a single origin of replication (oriC) and concludes at the replication terminus (ter). Due to mutational asymmetry, spontaneous deamination of cytosine to uracil on the single-stranded leading strand leads to an excess of Guanine over Cytosine.

$$\Delta S_{GC} = \frac{C - G}{C + G}$$

When scanning a circular bacterial genome in sliding windows, the cumulative GC-skew curve reaches a global minimum at the origin of replication ($oriC$) and a global maximum at the replication terminus ($ter$).
